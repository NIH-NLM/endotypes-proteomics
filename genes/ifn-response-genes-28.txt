# Interferon response genes, 28-gene score (union of Kim 2018 and de Jesus 2020).
# One HGNC gene symbol per line. Lines starting with # are notes.

# Sources
# Kim H, de Jesus AA, Brooks SR, et al. Development of a validated interferon score using NanoString
#   technology. J Interferon Cytokine Res 2018;38:171-185. Supplementary Table S1 (the 28 genes),
#   Supplementary Table S3 and Supplementary Figure S3A (the 25-gene score).
# de Jesus AA, Hou Y, Brooks S, et al. Distinct interferon signatures and cytokine patterns define
#   additional systemic autoinflammatory diseases. J Clin Invest 2020;130:1669-1682. Supplemental
#   Figure 3B (the 28 genes and their binding sites), Methods Step 2 and Supplemental Figure 5 (the 11
#   NF-kB-only genes).
#
# Differences between the two papers
#   The 28 genes are the same in both papers.
#   Each paper defines a different 25-gene subset:
#     Kim 2018: the 28 without EPSTI1, SIGLEC1 and SPATS2L, the three genes added from the literature.
#     de Jesus 2020: the 28 without CXCL10, GBP1 and SOCS1, the genes with STAT1 and NF-kB binding sites.
#   de Jesus 2020 marks two binding sites as uncertain: OAS3 NF-kB "Y?" and SIGLEC1 STAT1 "N?".
#   de Jesus 2020 states that SIGLEC1 was selected empirically for the score in Kim 2018.
#
